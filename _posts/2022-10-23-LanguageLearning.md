## Overview

HangulGo! is a mobile app designed to help students practice reading and writing Korean.

---

## Features

**Mutliple Choice Quizzes -** Themed question sets to build vocabulary and reading skills.  

**Handwriting Practice -** Integrated Google ML Kit Digital Ink Recognition, allowing users to write Korean characters on a canvas and receive real-time accuracy feedback.
